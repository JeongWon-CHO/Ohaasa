## English

Ohaasa does not require registration or login. On first launch, and for existing users who have not accepted the current terms, the iOS app presents a required terms agreement before onboarding or access to the app. The terms prohibit objectionable content and abusive behavior and describe content removal and restrictions for offending users.

To verify reporting and blocking:

1. Accept the terms and complete onboarding by selecting a zodiac sign.
2. Open the Community tab. Select the all-signs feed if necessary. A safe example answer begins with “[AI 예시]”. A comment from a separate example author begins with “[AI 댓글 예시]”. These examples are available for the latest broadcast date and use the same reporting and blocking mechanisms as ordinary content.
3. Tap the ellipsis on another author's answer or comment, select Report, choose a reason and confirm. Reports are submitted for operator review.
4. On an example from the other author, select Block user and confirm. That author's answers and comments disappear immediately and remain blocked after restarting the app. Blocking also queues the selected content for operator review; offline requests are retried on reconnect while the app is running.
5. If needed, unblock example authors through Settings > Community > Blocked users, which clears the local block list.

Public answers and comments are filtered before saving, including on server insert and update. Our operator reviews reports within 24 hours of receipt. When a violation is confirmed, the operator removes the author's content from public feeds and restricts that author's community posting. Developer contact is available in Settings > Contact developer and the privacy policy.

## 한국어 답변 초안

회원가입이나 로그인이 없는 앱이므로, 최초 실행 및 현행 약관에 동의하지 않은 기존 사용자의 앱 진입 전에 명시적인 약관 동의 화면을 추가했습니다. 부적절한 콘텐츠와 괴롭힘 무관용 정책, 신고 검토 및 위반 콘텐츠 제거·작성자 이용 제한을 안내합니다.

공개 답변·댓글의 작성과 수정에는 앱 및 서버 콘텐츠 필터가 적용됩니다. 답변과 댓글의 더보기에서 신고 및 작성자 차단이 가능하며, 차단 시 해당 작성자의 콘텐츠가 즉시 사라지고 운영자에게 검토 요청이 전달됩니다. 접수된 신고는 24시간 내에 검토하고, 위반이 확인되면 콘텐츠를 공개 피드에서 제거하고 작성자의 커뮤니티 작성 권한을 제한합니다.
